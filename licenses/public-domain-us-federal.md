# US federal government works: public domain

Data in this folder were produced by an agency of the United States federal government. Under 17 U.S.C. § 105,
works of the US Government are not subject to copyright in the United States, so they may be copied, shared and
reused without permission. Terms: https://www.usa.gov/government-works

Please still cite the publisher (each dataset card gives the citation), keep the publisher's suppression and
missing-value flags with the numbers, and do not imply that the agency endorses your work.

Some federal datasets carry their own terms; when they do, the dataset's catalog entry records them and its card
says so. This text was recorded by the health-data-registry maintainers from the terms page above.
